Introduction

Spree killers are similar to Serial killers but they do not have a cooling-off period between murders. They murder two or more people and the murders are normally at different locations. However some of the classifications can overlap.

Important Information

Motive

The killers can have many motives. One example is robbery. They can kill the person they are robbing because they need the money, and it causes them to kill the people around them. Another is sexual; the murderer can kill someone out of anger if they won’t cooperate, or if they are a witness to the crime. It is impossible to uncover the killer's true motive.

Evaluation

Evaluation is a big part of finding out how killers think. When experts evaluate a murder, they can change their opinion on whether the person has mental problems depending on their background. Their opinion can determine whether someone is criminally responsible. When the person being evaluated has a drug problem, they have a 63% chance of being evaluated as having a disorder.

Cases

Cumbria Incident

On June 2nd, 2010, over the course of two hours, Derrick Bird killed 12 people and injured 11. He started in the morning by killing his twin brother. Then he traveled through several towns, attacking many people that he did not know. This included the family solicitor, taxi drivers, and random citizens going about their day. The police were unable to respond fast enough because of poor communication between emergency services and the police force. Bird snapped because of a disagreement with his twin brother. He had tax-related issues, so he targeted the family’s solicitor. It is unknown why he killed so many random people, most likely because of mental issues.

Isla Vista Incident

On May 23rd, 2014, Elliot Rodger killed six people: three in his apartment, two outside of a sorority house, and one at a deli. Elliot Rodger was an outcast. He felt hatred toward women because he was unable to establish a romantic relationship. He also felt jealousy toward people who had friends, people who could talk to women romantically, and women in general. He wrote a 137-page paper describing his feelings and motives. He described wanting to kill people because it is so unfair that he couldn’t get a relationship, so other people should be punished for having them. He also had ties to the incel community. It is a community of men that hate women just because they can’t find a relationship, and it is no fault of their own. After committing the act, he became a substantial figure in the community, being called “Saint Elliot”.
